About

Revytal started in real PT clinics, where product recommendations were already happening.

Clinicians recommend products because patients need them. Revytal exists because the next step should not depend on a retail search, a scribbled product name, or a patient guessing which item was meant.

What we protect

The relationship comes first: clinician, patient, recommendation, follow-through. Revytal keeps that loop protected, not louder.
