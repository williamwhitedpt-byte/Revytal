How It Works

Five steps. No retail detour.

Revytal keeps the product recommendation attached to the person who made it, all the way through checkout and order handoff.

The point

Patients should not need to become product researchers just because they were told to buy a brace.

01

Clinician chooses the product.

The product comes from the clinic catalog, not an open marketplace search.

02

Revytal builds the handoff.

The patient gets a link or QR code with the exact product and a clear reason.

03

Patient checks out.

The page stays focused on the recommended item, sizing, and purchase path.

04

Order routes for fulfillment.

Revytal handles the commerce layer while the clinic keeps moving.

The checkout flow should feel uneventful.

That is the point. The patient sees the product, understands why it was recommended, and completes purchase without turning the visit into a product-support session.

Prototype flow for team review. If this video fails locally, check the shared landing folder asset.

After purchase

Visibility first. Outcomes later.

The immediate promise is purchase visibility and a cleaner handoff. Product-specific follow-up and outcomes signals belong to the next layer, not the top-line claim.

Claim hygiene

Say Revytal can make follow-through visible. Do not say it improves outcomes until real pilot evidence can carry that weight.
