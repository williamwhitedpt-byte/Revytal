What Revytal is, in the language of a normal treatment day.

Revytal helps a clinic send patients to the exact product recommended during care. The clinician does not become a salesperson. The clinic does not become a warehouse. The patient gets a cleaner next step.

Recommend

Choose from an approved product set when the recommendation already belongs in the visit.

Share

Send the patient a QR code or link tied to the clinic and the product.

Revytal handles

Checkout path, order routing, product movement visibility, and support boundaries.

Clinic handles

Clinical judgment, patient context, and feedback on whether the workflow fits real practice.
