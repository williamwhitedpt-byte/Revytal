Economics

Revenue is real. It just should not be the first thing a patient hears.

Revytal can help clinics capture appropriate margin from recommendations they already influence. For clinics, trust comes first, because nobody wants their PT visit to feel like a checkout aisle.

Two audiences, one product.

Owners see margin. Clinicians see a cleaner handoff. The product respects both.

Illustrative math, not a forecast.

These numbers help a clinic owner picture the opportunity. The honest claim is simple: product recommendations already create value, and Revytal gives clinics a cleaner way to participate.

What stays true

No product-pushing. No promised monthly number. No turning clinicians into salespeople. Just operational hygiene with upside.
