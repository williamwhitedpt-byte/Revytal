Economics

Revenue is real. It just should not be the first thing a patient hears.

Revytal can help clinics capture appropriate margin from recommendations they already influence. The clinic-facing story still starts with trust, because nobody wants their PT visit to feel like a checkout aisle.

Positioning rule

For owners, talk about margin. For clinicians, talk about a cleaner handoff. Same product, different pressure.

01

Existing demand

Clinicians already recommend braces, bands, supports, topicals, and recovery products.

02

Preferred sourcing

Supplier pricing creates the potential margin pool, subject to actual terms and product mix.

03

Shared value

Patient pays through a focused path; Revytal and the clinic participate in appropriate margin.

Keep the math illustrative until pilot data earns more.

Early models can help a clinic owner understand the opportunity, but public copy should avoid pretending one sample spreadsheet is destiny. The safer claim is simple: product recommendations already create value, and Revytal gives clinics a cleaner way to participate.

What to avoid

Do not lead with product-pushing language. Do not promise a monthly number. Do not frame clinicians as salespeople. The product is strongest when it feels like operational hygiene with upside.
