Best fit

Clinics that already recommend braces, bands, supports, or recovery products and want patients to stop guessing which one was meant.

The recommendation is clinical. The purchase path should not feel random.

The current handoff asks the patient to remember, search, compare, and choose. Revytal narrows the path: exact product, clinic-branded checkout, fulfillment routing, and a simple signal back to the clinic.

What changes

  • Patients get one focused path instead of a search result page.
  • Clinics avoid inventory, shelves, and return-desk chaos.
  • The product movement stays connected to the visit.
