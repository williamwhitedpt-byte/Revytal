The leak is not clinical. It is infrastructural.

PTs create trusted product demand every day, then watch the purchase happen somewhere else. Revytal keeps that value inside the clinic ecosystem without asking the team to run retail.

01

03

Built around the way PT clinics already work.

Revytal turns a normal clinical recommendation into a curated checkout link, then routes payment, fulfillment, and clinic revenue in the background.

Step 01+

Recommend as usual.

The provider names the brace, topical, supplement, orthotic, or recovery tool that fits the patient's plan.

Step 02+

Revytal prepares the link.

Products are matched from a curated clinical catalog and packaged into a clean, clinic-branded checkout.

Step 03+

The patient buys directly.

The patient receives a QR code or SMS link with the exact products, plain-language context, and a fast checkout.

Step 04+

Revenue returns to the clinic.

Suppliers ship directly. Revytal logs the transaction. The clinic participates in value it already created.
