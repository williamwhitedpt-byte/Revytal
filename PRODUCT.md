# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Primary users are PT and rehab clinic owners, PT directors, practice operators, and clinicians evaluating whether Revytal can make product recommendations easier and safer to hand off. Secondary users are investors, advisors, and internal reviewers comparing strategy, page structure, and launch readiness.

## Product Purpose

Revytal gives clinics a trusted, clinic-branded path from product recommendation to patient purchase. The site should explain the broken handoff first, show that Revytal keeps the recommendation connected to the clinic relationship, and invite qualified clinics into a careful pilot without making clinicians feel like salespeople.

## Brand Personality

Calm, clinical, practical, modern, and lightly human. Clinic-facing pages should feel warmer and more conversational than investor pages, while still respecting clinical seriousness and operational boundaries.

## Anti-references

Avoid public copy that reads like strategy notes, design commentary, internal positioning, or investor thesis language on clinic-facing pages. Avoid product catalog framing, retail storefront energy, affiliate-marketing language, sterile SaaS boilerplate, excessive coral, and repeated template-like card grids.

## Design Principles

- Lead with care continuity before revenue.
- Make the product handoff concrete and easy to picture.
- Use delight as proof of craft, not decoration.
- Keep coral rare, memorable, and connected to emphasis or state change.
- Use the Eighties Comeback font only for short emphasized words, usually in coral.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Respect reduced-motion preferences, preserve readable contrast, avoid horizontal overflow, provide alt text for screenshots and assets, and keep interactive review/demo elements usable by keyboard and screen readers.
